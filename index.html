01 / THE CHALLENGE

Parking shouldn’t be a guessing game.

Drivers heading to busy markets, offices, colleges, and hospitals can lose time circling streets or arriving at full lots. Information about availability, pricing, and restrictions is often fragmented.

Problem statement
Urban drivers need a convenient, trustworthy way to compare nearby parking options before they arrive.

04 / WHAT WE LEARNED

From pain points to opportunities

01

Uncertain availability

Show availability with a last-updated time and clearly label estimates.

02

Unexpected costs

Present parking rates, payment methods, and restrictions before selection.

03

Confusing arrival

Include the entrance location, walking distance, and turn-by-turn directions.

04

Different accessibility needs

Provide filters for accessible, covered, and short-walk parking options.

05 / NEXT STEP

Validate. Prototype. Improve.

Interview 5–8 drivers, test these assumptions, and build a low-fidelity ParkEase prototype around the most important needs.
